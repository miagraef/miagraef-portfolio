PASSIONATE CREATIVE DRIVEN BY COMMUNITY

My name is Mia Graef and I am an undergraduate student studying Graphic Information Technology. Through both Graphic Design and UX/UI Design, I appreciate both the craft and the community. My goal is to innovate creatively with authentic designs that can spread the word, gather people together, and bring thoughtful joy to those who see and consume my art. Along with design through technology, I am equally as passionate about fibre arts, ceramics, and sewing, and can offer a multitude of mixed media.
